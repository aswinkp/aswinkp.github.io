## Variant: Editorial Founder Memo

### Design stance
A warm, editorial founder page that reads like a living operating memo rather than a résumé.

### Key choices
- Layout: Founder letter + side table-of-contents + portfolio cards.
- Typography: Serif display with warm neutral Inter body.
- Color: Paper-like whites and browns, almost no saturated color.
- Interaction: Contact CTA toast.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: trust, humanity, founder narrative, long-form writing.
- Weak at: less immediately “startup dashboard” or high-tech.

### Best for
- Visitors who should understand your story, thinking, values, and why your product portfolio exists.
